4. What the Software Does

Heat Watch reads your Mac's CPU, GPU, memory, power, temperature, battery, network and disk figures, shows them in the menu bar and its window, and alerts you when something keeps the Mac working hard. Its figures are estimates from what macOS reports; they are informational and not a diagnostic or safety tool.

Quitting apps can lose unsaved work. When you choose Quit or Stop Gradle Daemons and confirm, Heat Watch asks those processes to stop.
